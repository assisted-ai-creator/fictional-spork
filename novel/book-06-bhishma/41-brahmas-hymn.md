# Brahma's Hymn

Dhritarashtra said, "Great fear has come over me, Sanjaya, and wonder too, at
hearing of the deeds of Pandu's young sons, deeds very hard even for the gods.
And hearing of my sons' defeat on every side, Sanjaya, I am very anxious,
charioteer. How will it turn out? Surely Vidura's words will burn my heart, for
everything is seen to happen by the working of fate, Sanjaya. There brave men
led by Bhishma, who know weapons, the best of fighters, hard hitters, fight the
Pandava ranks. Why can the great-souled, very strong sons of Pandu not be
killed? Who gave them boons, son? Or what knowledge do they have, that they are
not destroyed, like the hosts of stars in the sky? Again and again I cannot bear
to hear that my army has been killed by the Pandavas. A most cruel punishment
falls from fate on me alone. How is it that Pandu's sons cannot be killed, and
how is it that my sons can be? Tell me all this as it truly is, Sanjaya. I see
no farther shore to this grief at all, any more than a man swimming the great
ocean with his arms. I think a most cruel disaster has surely come on my sons.
Bhima will kill all my sons, there is no doubt. I see no hero who could protect
my sons in battle. My sons' destruction in battle is certain, Sanjaya. So,
charioteer, you should tell me everything as it truly is, the reason and above
all the logic of it, now that I ask. What did Duryodhana do when he saw his own
men turn away in battle? What did Bhishma and Drona, Kripa, Subala's son and
Jayadratha do, or the great archer Drona's son, or the very strong Vikarna? What
did those great-souled ones decide when my sons turned away, very wise Sanjaya?"

"Listen carefully, king," said Sanjaya, "and when you have heard, take it to
heart. The Pandavas use no spells, king, no such illusions, no kind of terror.
They fight properly, and they are strong in battle. The Parthas always begin all
their tasks by dharma, as it is laid down, Bharata, seeking great glory. They do
not turn back from battle. They are very strong, possessed of dharma and filled
with the highest fortune. Where dharma is, there is victory. That is why the
Parthas cannot be killed in battle and are victorious, king. Your sons are
evil-hearted, always delighting in wrong. They are harsh and do low deeds, and
that is why they lose in battle. Your sons, like base men, have done many cruel
deeds and wrongs to the Pandavas here, lord of the people. Disregarding all that
wrong of your sons, the Pandavas always kept it hidden, elder brother of Pandu.
And your sons do not think much of them, lord of the people. Now the fruit of
that wrong deed, done again and again, has come, very great and terrible, like
the kimpaka fruit. Eat it, great king, with your sons and your friends. You did
not understand, king, when your friends held you back, Vidura and Bhishma and
the great-souled Drona. I too held you back again and again, and you did not
take the good and wholesome advice, as a dying man does not take wholesome
medicine. You held to your sons' opinion and thought the Pandavas were beaten.

"Hear further, as it truly is, what you ask me: the reason for the Pandavas'
victory, best of the Bharatas. I will tell it to you as I heard it, tamer of
enemies. Duryodhana asked the grandfather about this. Seeing all his brothers,
great chariot fighters, beaten in battle, the Kaurava, his heart bewildered with
grief, went humbly to the very wise grandfather at nightfall. Hear what your son
said, lord of the people.

"Duryodhana said, 'You, Drona and Shalya, Kripa and Drona's son, Kritavarma son
of Hridika and Sudakshina of Kamboja, Bhurishravas, Vikarna and the mighty
Bhagadatta are famed as great chariot fighters, of good family, men who will
give up their bodies. My view is that you are enough for the three worlds. All
the Pandavas together do not stand up to your courage. So a doubt has come to
me. Tell me, since I ask. On whom do the Kaunteyas rely, that they defeat us at
every step?'

"Bhishma said, 'Hear my words, king, what I will say to you, Kaurava. I have
told you many times, and you have not done what I said. Make peace with the
Pandavas, best of the Bharatas. That is what I think good for the earth and for
you, lord. Enjoy this earth happily with your brothers, king, tormenting all who
wish you ill and delighting your kin. But you did not listen to me before, son,
when I cried out to you. Now this has come, because you despise the Pandavas.
Hear, great king, the reason why those of tireless deeds cannot be killed, as I
tell it, lord. There is no being in the worlds, there never was and there never
will be, who could defeat the Pandavas in battle while they are protected by the
bearer of the Sharnga bow. Hear exactly, son, you who know dharma, what seers of
perfected self told me, the old story that has been sung.

"'Long ago, they say, all the gods and the seers came together and sat in
attendance on the grandfather on Mount Gandhamadana. Seated among them,
Prajapati saw a splendid aerial chariot standing in the sky, blazing with light.
Knowing by meditation who it was, Brahma joined his palms with self-control and,
glad at heart, bowed to the supreme, highest lord. The seers and the gods,
seeing Brahma rise, all stood with joined palms, looking at that great wonder.
Brahma, the best of those who know brahman, the creator of the world, who knows
the highest dharma, honoured him as was fitting and spoke to the Highest.

"'"You are the wealth of all, the form of all, the lord of all, Vishvaksena, the
maker of all, the self-controlled. You are the lord of all, Vasudeva. So I come
to you, the god whose self is yoga. Victory to you, the All, the great god!
Victory to you, intent on the good of the worlds! Victory, lord of yogins,
mighty one! Victory, you who are before and beyond yoga! You with the lotus
within, wide-eyed one, victory, lord of the lords of the worlds! Lord of what
was, what will be and what is, victory, gentle one, son of your own self!
Unconquered, with qualities beyond counting, victory, refuge of all! Narayana,
very hard to cross, victory, bearer of the Sharnga bow! You with every secret
quality, form of all, free from sickness, lord of all, strong-armed, victory,
you who are intent on the good of the world! Great serpent, first boar,
tawny-haired one, mighty one, victory! You clothed in yellow, lord of the
people, dwelling place of all, measureless, unchanging! You whose place is
manifest, unmanifest and measureless, with senses controlled and with senses,
who know the selves beyond counting, victory, deep one, granter of desires!
Endless one, of known wisdom, always bringing beings into being, you whose work
is done, of perfect wisdom, knower of dharma, victory and the unconquered!
Secret self, self of all beings, source of all that is clearly born, the truth
of the meaning of beings, lord of the world, victory, bringer of beings into
being! Self-born one, most fortunate, intent on the destruction of the eon, you
who bring forth, born of mind, victory, lover of brahmins! You who delight in
creating and dissolving, lord of desire, highest lord, source of the deathless,
true being, fire at the end of the age, giver of victory! Lord of Prajapati,
god, lotus-naveled, very strong, self of beings, great being, self of action,
victory, giver of action!

"'"The goddess Earth is your feet, the quarters your arms, the sky your head. I
am your form, the gods your body, the moon and sun your eyes. Strength,
austerity, truth and dharma are born of your desire, lord. Fire is your
splendour, the wind your breath, the waters are born of your sweat. The Ashvins
are always your ears, the goddess Sarasvati your tongue, and the Vedas are your
sacred rites. On you this world rests. We do not know your number, your measure,
your splendour, your courage or your strength, lord of the yoga of yoga, nor
your origin. Devoted to you, god, fixed on you by our vows, we always worship
you, Vishnu, the highest lord, the great lord. Seers, gods, gandharvas, yakshas,
rakshasas, serpents, pishachas, men, beasts, birds and creeping things: all
these I created on the earth, born from your grace. Lotus-naveled, wide-eyed
Krishna, destroyer of bad dreams, you are the goal of all beings. You are the
leader, you are the mouth of the world. Through your grace, lord of the gods,
the gods are always happy. Through your grace, god, the earth has always been
free from fear. So, wide-eyed one, become the one who makes the line of the
Yadus prosper. To establish dharma, to kill the sons of Diti, to uphold the
world, do what I ask, lord.

"'"This is the highest secret, mighty one, which comes from your grace,
Vasudeva. I have sung it as it truly is. You created the god Samkarshana, your
own self, by yourself. Krishna, from yourself you created Pradyumna, born of
yourself. From Pradyumna you created Aniruddha, whom they know as the unchanging
Vishnu. Aniruddha created me, Brahma, who hold up the worlds. So I am made of
Vasudeva, created by you alone. Divide yourself into parts, lord, and go into
human form. There you will kill the asuras for the happiness of all the worlds,
establish dharma and win glory, and you will truly reach yoga again. For brahmin
seers in the world and gods, you of measureless courage, sing of you, devoted,
as the highest self, with all those names. All the hosts of beings rest in you,
taking refuge in you, the giver of boons, strong-armed one. Brahmins say you are
without beginning, middle or end, of boundless yoga, the bridge of the world."'"
