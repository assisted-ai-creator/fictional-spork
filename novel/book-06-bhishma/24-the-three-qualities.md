# The Three Qualities

"'I will tell you again the highest knowledge,' said the Blessed One, 'the best
of all knowledge, knowing which all the sages have gone from here to the highest
perfection. Taking refuge in this knowledge, they have come to be like me. They
are not born even at the creation, nor troubled at the dissolution. My womb is
the great Brahman. In it I place the seed, and from it all beings come to be,
Bharata. Whatever forms come to be in all wombs, son of Kunti, the great Brahman
is their womb, and I am the father who gives the seed.

"'Goodness, passion and darkness: these qualities born of nature bind the
undying embodied one in the body, strong-armed one. Of these, goodness, being
stainless, gives light and is free of sickness. It binds by attachment to
happiness and attachment to knowledge, sinless one. Know that passion is made of
desire, born of craving and attachment. It binds the embodied one, son of Kunti,
by attachment to action. But know that darkness is born of ignorance, confusing
all the embodied. It binds, Bharata, by carelessness, sloth and sleep. Goodness
attaches to happiness, passion to action, Bharata. But darkness, covering
knowledge, attaches to carelessness.

"'Goodness comes to be by overcoming passion and darkness, Bharata. Passion
comes to be by overcoming goodness and darkness, and darkness by overcoming
goodness and passion. When light, which is knowledge, arises at all the gates of
this body, then one should know that goodness has grown strong. Greed, activity,
the undertaking of actions, restlessness and longing arise when passion grows
strong, bull of the Bharatas. Darkness, inactivity, carelessness and confusion
arise when darkness grows strong, joy of the Kurus.

"'When the embodied one meets dissolution while goodness is strong, he goes to
the stainless worlds of those who know the highest. Meeting dissolution in
passion, he is born among those attached to action. Dissolved in darkness, he is
born in the wombs of the confused. They say the fruit of good action is good and
stainless. The fruit of passion is pain, and the fruit of darkness ignorance.
From goodness knowledge is born, from passion greed, from darkness carelessness,
confusion and ignorance too. Those who stand in goodness go upward. Those of
passion stay in the middle. Those of darkness, standing in the working of the
lowest quality, go downward.

"'When the seer sees no doer other than the qualities, and knows what is beyond
the qualities, he comes to my state. When the embodied one goes beyond these
three qualities, which give rise to the body, he is freed from birth, death, old
age and pain, and reaches immortality.'

"'By what marks is one known who has gone beyond these three qualities, lord?'
said Arjuna. 'How does he behave, and how does he go beyond them?'

"'He does not hate light, activity or confusion when they are present, Pandava,
nor long for them when they are gone,' said the Blessed One. 'He sits as one
indifferent and is not shaken by the qualities. He stays firm and does not
waver, thinking, "It is only the qualities at work." He is the same in pain and
pleasure, at ease in himself, and a clod, a stone and gold are alike to him. He
is steady, the same towards the dear and the not dear, the same in blame and
praise of himself. He is the same in honour and dishonour, the same to the side
of friend and enemy, and he has given up every undertaking. Such a man is said
to have gone beyond the qualities. And one who serves me with the yoga of
devotion that never strays goes beyond these qualities and is fit to become
Brahman. For I am the foundation of Brahman, of the immortal and undying, of
eternal dharma, and of perfect happiness.'"
