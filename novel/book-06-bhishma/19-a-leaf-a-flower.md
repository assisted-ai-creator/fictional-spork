# A Leaf, a Flower

"'Now I will tell you, since you do not find fault,' said the Blessed One, 'this
most secret knowledge, with discernment. When you know it, you will be freed
from evil. It is the royal knowledge, the royal secret, the highest purifier,
known by direct sight, in keeping with dharma, very easy to practise, and
undying. Men who have no faith in this dharma, scorcher of enemies, do not reach
me, and return to the path of death and wandering.

"'All this world is pervaded by me in my unmanifest form. All beings stand in
me, and I do not stand in them. And yet beings do not stand in me. See my lordly
yoga. My self supports beings and brings them into being, but does not stand in
them. As the great wind, going everywhere, always stands in space, so understand
that all beings stand in me. At the end of an age, son of Kunti, all beings go
into my nature, and at the beginning of an age I send them forth again. Resting
on my own nature, I send forth again and again this whole host of beings,
helpless, by the power of nature. And those actions do not bind me, Dhananjaya,
for I sit as one indifferent, unattached to those actions. With me as overseer,
nature gives birth to the moving and the unmoving. For this reason, son of
Kunti, the world turns.

"'The confused despise me when I take on a human body, not knowing my higher
state as the great lord of beings. Their hopes are vain, their actions vain,
their knowledge vain, and they are senseless, given to the confusing nature of
rakshasas and asuras. But great souls, Partha, given to the divine nature,
worship me with minds turned to nothing else, knowing me as the undying
beginning of beings. Always praising me, striving, firm in their vows, bowing to
me with devotion, always disciplined, they worship me. Others too worship me,
sacrificing with the sacrifice of knowledge, as one, as separate, as many,
facing every way.

"'I am the rite, I am the sacrifice, I am the offering to the ancestors, I am
the healing herb. I am the mantra, I am the clarified butter, I am the fire, I
am the offering. I am the father of this world, the mother, the upholder, the
grandfather, what is to be known, the purifier, the syllable Om, the Rig, the
Saman and the Yajus too. I am the goal, the supporter, the lord, the witness,
the home, the refuge, the friend, the origin, the dissolution, the resting
place, the storehouse, the undying seed. I give heat. I hold back the rain and
send it forth. I am immortality and death, being and non-being, Arjuna.

"'Those who know the three Vedas, who drink the soma and are cleansed of sin,
sacrifice to me with sacrifices and pray for the way to heaven. Reaching the
holy world of the lord of the gods, they enjoy the divine pleasures of the gods
in heaven. When they have enjoyed that wide world of heaven and their merit is
spent, they enter the world of mortals. So those who follow the dharma of the
three Vedas, desiring desires, get going and coming. But those men who think of
me, turned to nothing else, and worship me, always disciplined, I carry their
getting and their keeping.

"'Even those who are devoted to other gods and sacrifice with faith, son of
Kunti, sacrifice to me alone, though not by the rule. For I am the enjoyer and
the lord of all sacrifices. But they do not know me truly, and so they fall.
Those vowed to the gods go to the gods. Those vowed to the ancestors go to the
ancestors. Those who sacrifice to spirits go to the spirits. Those who sacrifice
to me come to me.

"'Whoever offers me with devotion a leaf, a flower, a fruit or water, that
offering of devotion I accept from one whose self is controlled. Whatever you
do, whatever you eat, whatever you offer in sacrifice, whatever you give,
whatever austerity you practise, son of Kunti, do it as an offering to me. So
you will be freed from the bonds of action, with their good and bad fruits. Your
self disciplined by the yoga of renouncing, freed, you will come to me.

"'I am the same to all beings. No one is hateful to me, and no one dear. But
those who worship me with devotion are in me, and I am in them. Even if a man of
very evil conduct worships me with devotion to no other, he must be thought
good, for he has resolved rightly. He soon becomes dharmic in soul and goes to
lasting peace. Know this, son of Kunti: my devotee does not perish. For even
those who may be of evil birth, women, vaishyas and shudras too, if they take
refuge in me, Partha, reach the highest end. How much more the holy brahmins and
devoted royal seers. Since you have come to this world, which does not last and
has no happiness, worship me. Let your mind be on me, be devoted to me,
sacrifice to me, bow to me. Disciplining yourself like this, intent on me, you
will come to me.'"
