# The Field and Its Knower

"'This body, son of Kunti,' said the Blessed One, 'is called the field. Those
who know it call one who knows it the knower of the field. Know me too as the
knower of the field in all fields, Bharata. The knowledge of the field and its
knower: that I hold to be knowledge. Hear from me in brief what that field is,
what it is like, how it changes and where it comes from, and who he is and what
his power is. It has been sung by the seers in many ways, separately in many
metres, and in the words of the Brahma Sutras, full of reasons and settled.

"'The great elements, self-regard, understanding and the unmanifest, the ten
senses and the one, and the five fields of the senses: these make the field.
Desire, hatred, pleasure, pain, the body, consciousness and steadiness belong to
it. This, in brief, is the field with its changes.

"'Freedom from pride and deceit, not harming, patience, uprightness, service to
the teacher, purity, steadiness and self-control are knowledge. So are
detachment from the objects of the senses, freedom from self-regard, and seeing
the evil of birth, death, old age, sickness and pain. So are freedom from
attachment, not clinging to son, wife, home and the rest, and a mind always the
same in what is wanted and unwanted. So are devotion to me that never strays,
with yoga given to nothing else, resorting to lonely places and taking no
pleasure in the crowd. So are constant knowledge of the self and seeing the
purpose of the knowledge of truth. This is called knowledge. What is other than
this is ignorance.

"'I will tell you what is to be known, by knowing which one reaches immortality.
It is the beginningless highest Brahman, said to be neither being nor non-being.
Its hands and feet are everywhere, its eyes, heads and mouths are everywhere,
its ears everywhere. It stands covering all in the world. It shines with the
qualities of all the senses, yet is free of all the senses. Unattached, it
supports all. Without qualities, it enjoys the qualities. It is outside and
inside beings, unmoving and moving. It is too subtle to be known. It is far away
and it is near. Undivided, it seems divided among beings. It is to be known as
the supporter of beings, their devourer and their creator. It is the light of
lights, said to be beyond darkness. It is knowledge, what is to be known, and
what is reached by knowledge, set in the heart of all. So the field, knowledge
and what is to be known have been told in brief. My devotee, knowing this, comes
to my state.

"'Know that nature and the Person are both without beginning, and know that the
changes and the qualities are born of nature. Nature is said to be the cause in
the making of cause and effect. The Person is said to be the cause in the
experiencing of pleasure and pain. For the Person, standing in nature, enjoys
the qualities born of nature. Attachment to the qualities is the cause of his
births in good and evil wombs. The highest Person in this body is called the
witness, the one who consents, the supporter, the enjoyer, the great lord, and
the highest self. One who knows the Person and nature with its qualities like
this is not born again, however he lives.

"'Some see the self in the self by the self through meditation, others by the
yoga of Samkhya, others by the yoga of action. Others, not knowing this, hear it
from others and worship. They too, devoted to what they have heard, cross beyond
death. Whatever being is born, unmoving or moving, know that it comes from the
union of the field and its knower, bull of the Bharatas.

"'One who sees the highest lord standing the same in all beings, not perishing
when they perish, sees. Seeing the lord standing the same everywhere, he does
not harm the self by the self, and so goes to the highest end. One who sees that
all actions are done by nature alone, and the self as not the doer, sees. When
he sees the separate states of beings as standing in one, and their spreading
out from that alone, then he reaches Brahman.

"'Because it is without beginning and without qualities, this highest self,
undying, though it stands in the body, son of Kunti, does not act and is not
stained. As space, going everywhere, is not stained, because it is so subtle, so
the self, standing everywhere in the body, is not stained. As the one sun lights
up this whole world, so the owner of the field lights up the whole field,
Bharata. Those who know with the eye of knowledge the difference between the
field and its knower, and the freeing of beings from nature, go to the
highest.'"
