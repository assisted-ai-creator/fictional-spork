# The Divine and the Demonic

"'Fearlessness, purity of being, steadiness in the yoga of knowledge,' said the
Blessed One, 'giving, self-control and sacrifice, study of the Veda, austerity
and uprightness belong to one born to the divine lot. So do not harming, truth,
freedom from anger, renunciation, peace, freedom from slander, compassion for
beings and freedom from greed. So do gentleness, modesty, steadiness, splendour,
patience, firmness, purity, freedom from malice and from too much pride. These
belong to one born to the divine lot, Bharata. Hypocrisy, arrogance, too much
pride, anger, harshness and ignorance belong to one born to the demonic lot,
Partha. The divine lot is held to lead to release, the demonic to bondage. Do
not grieve, Pandava. You are born to the divine lot.

"'There are two kinds of beings created in this world, the divine and the
demonic. The divine has been told in full. Hear from me of the demonic, Partha.
Demonic men do not know what to do and what to refrain from. Neither purity nor
good conduct nor truth is found in them. They say the world has no truth and no
foundation and no lord, that it is not born of one thing after another. What
else? It is caused by desire. Holding this view, lost souls of little
understanding, of fierce deeds, they rise up as enemies for the destruction of
the world. Taking refuge in desire, which is hard to fill, full of hypocrisy,
pride and drunkenness, taking up false notions through confusion, they go about
with impure vows. They are given over to endless care that ends only at death,
intent on enjoying desires, sure that this is all. Bound by hundreds of nooses
of hope, devoted to desire and anger, they strive to pile up wealth by wrong
means for the enjoyment of their desires.

"'"This I have gained today. This wish I will fulfil. This is mine, and this
wealth too will be mine. I have killed that enemy, and I will kill others too. I
am the lord, I am the enjoyer, I am successful, strong and happy. I am rich and
well born. Who else is like me? I will sacrifice, I will give, I will rejoice."
So they think, confused by ignorance. Bewildered by many thoughts, covered in a
net of confusion, attached to the enjoyment of desires, they fall into a foul
hell. Self-admiring, stubborn, full of the pride and drunkenness of wealth, they
sacrifice with sacrifices in name only, with hypocrisy, not by the rule. Given
to self-regard, force, arrogance, desire and anger, they hate me in their own
bodies and in others', finding fault. Those cruel haters, the lowest of men,
evil, I hurl again and again in the wanderings into demonic wombs alone. Fallen
into demonic wombs, the confused, birth after birth, never reaching me, son of
Kunti, go from there to the lowest end.

"'This is the threefold gate of hell, the destroyer of the self: desire, anger
and greed. So one should give up these three. A man freed from these three gates
of darkness, son of Kunti, does what is best for himself, and so goes to the
highest end. One who casts aside the rule of the science of dharma and acts as
desire moves him reaches neither perfection nor happiness nor the highest end.
So let the science be your measure in deciding what should be done and what
should not. Knowing what is said in the rule of the science, you should do your
work here.'"

**Choices.**

* 6.38.3 *saṃpadaṃ daivīm*: "the divine lot", one's endowment at birth.
* 6.38.8 *aparasparasaṃbhūtam*: "not born of one thing after another", a hard phrase; some read it "born of mutual union". The rendering follows the words.
* 6.38.17 *nāmayajñaiḥ*: "sacrifices in name only".
* 6.38.24 *śāstra*: "the science of dharma", the body of sacred teaching on conduct.
