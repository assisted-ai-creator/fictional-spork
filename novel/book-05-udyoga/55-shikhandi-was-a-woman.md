# Shikhandi Was a Woman

"'The sons of Draupadi, great king, all five, are great chariot fighters,'" said
Bhishma. "'Virata's son Uttara I think a great ratha. Abhimanyu, great king,
leader of the leaders of chariot troops, could be equal in battle to Partha or
to Vasudeva. Light with his weapons, a fighter of many skills, high-spirited and
firm in courage, he will show his courage remembering his father's torment.
Satyaki of the Madhu line, a hero, leader of the leaders of chariot troops, is
the impatient, fearless one among the Vrishni heroes. Uttamaujas too, king, I
think a great ratha, and valiant Yudhamanyu a fine chariot fighter, a bull among
men. They have many thousands of chariots, elephants and horses. They will
fight, giving up their bodies, to please Kunti's son, with the Pandavas, king of
kings, against your armies, Bharata, calling each other on like fire and wind,
king.

"'Virata and Drupada, both old and unconquerable in battle, I think great
chariot fighters of great courage, bulls among men. Though they are old, they
are devoted to the dharma of a kshatriya and stand on the road heroes walk. They
will strive with all their power. Because of their kinship, king of kings, and
by their courage, strength and birth, those two great archers of noble conduct
are both bound by the noose of love. All strong-armed men, bull among men,
become brave or cowards when they find a cause. These two have gone the one way,
firm in their devotion to Partha, and will give up their lives and strive with
all their power, lord of men. Each with his own army, both terrible in battle,
they will do great deeds, guarding their kinship. Heroes of the world, great
archers ready to give up their lives, Bharata, they will do great deeds,
guarding the trust placed in them.

---

"'The son of the Panchala king, king, Shikhandi, conqueror of enemy cities, I
think one of Partha's chief chariot fighters, Bharata. He will fight in battle,
destroying his former state and spreading his fame far among your armies,
Bharata. He has many troops, Panchalas and Prabhadrakas, and with that line of
chariots he will do great deeds. Dhrishtadyumna, the general of all the armies,
Bharata, I think an atiratha, king, Drona's pupil, a great chariot fighter. He
will fight in battle, slaying the enemy, like the blessed god who holds the
Pinaka bow in his anger at the end of the age. Those who love battle say his
line of chariots is like the sea, it is so large, like the gods' in battle. But
Kshatradharman, king of kings, Dhrishtadyumna's son, I think half a ratha, king.
He is a child and has not trained enough.

"'Shishupala's son, the hero, the Chedi king, the great chariot fighter
Dhrishtaketu, the great archer, is a kinsman of the Pandava. This brave lord of
the Chedis, Bharata, with his son, a great chariot fighter, will do a great deed
that is hard to do. Kshatradeva, conqueror of enemy cities, devoted to the
dharma of a kshatriya, I think a fine chariot fighter among the Pandavas, king
of kings, and Jayanta, Amitaujas and the great chariot fighter Satyajit. All
those great men, great chariot fighters, the best of the Panchalas, will fight
in battle, my son, enraged like elephants. Aja and Bhoja, both valiant, great
chariot fighters among the Pandavas, will strive with all their power to help
the Pandavas. They are quick with weapons, fighters of many skills, practised
and firm in courage.

"'The five Kekaya brothers, king of kings, fierce in war, all fine chariot
fighters, all with red banners. Kashika, Sukumara and Nila, and another king,
Suryadatta, Shankha and the one named Madirashva, all fine chariot fighters, all
marked for battle, all knowing every weapon, all great men, I think.
Vardhakshemi, great king, I think a great ratha, and King Chitrayudha a fine
chariot fighter. He shines in battle and is devoted to the diademed one.
Chekitana and Satyadhriti are great chariot fighters of the Pandavas. Those two
tigers among men I think fine chariot fighters. Vyaghradatta, king of kings, and
Chandrasena, Bharata, I think fine chariot fighters of the Pandavas, no doubt of
it. Senabindu, king of kings, also named Krodhahanta, who is a match for
Vasudeva and Bhimasena, lord, will fight here bravely in battle against your
soldiers. As you regard me, Drona and Kripa, so should you regard him, proud in
battle, a fine chariot fighter. The man of Kashi, very quick with weapons, a
fine chariot fighter worth praise, conqueror of enemy cities, I think a ratha
worth one. And this young son of Drupada, Satyajit, valiant in war, proud in
battle, should be thought a ratha worth eight. He has become an atiratha, a
match for Dhrishtadyumna. Wanting fame for the Pandavas, he will do great deeds.
Another great ratha, loyal and brave, is the Pandya king of great courage, who
carries the Pandavas' burden. Dridhadhanva, the great archer, is a fine chariot
fighter of the Pandavas. Shreniman, best of the Kauravas, and King Vasudana I
think both atirathas, scorcher of enemies.

---

"'Rochamana, great king, a great chariot fighter of the Pandavas, will fight
like an immortal in battle among the enemy armies, Bharata. Purujit Kuntibhoja,
the great archer of great strength, Bhimasena's uncle, I think an atiratha too.
That hero is a great archer, practised and skilled, a fighter of many skills and
able. I think him a bull among chariot fighters. He will fight bravely, like
Maghavat against the danavas, and his fighters are famous, all skilled in war.
That hero, for his sister's sons, devoted to the good of Pandu's sons, will do
very great deeds in battle, king. Bhimasena's son, great king, Hidimba's son,
the lord of rakshasas, I think a master of magic, leader of the leaders of
chariot troops. He will fight in battle with his magic, my son, loving battle,
and so will his brave rakshasa counsellors, who obey him.

"'These and many others, lords of many countries, have gathered for the
Pandava's sake, with Vasudeva at their head. These are the chief of the great
Pandava's rathas, king, his atirathas and those thought half rathas. They will
lead Yudhishthira's terrible army in battle, king, guarded by the diademed hero
as by great Indra. In battle, hero, I will fight them as they come at you
seeking victory, wanting victory or death in battle. I will meet Partha and
Vasudeva, bearers of the discus and Gandiva, the best of men, like the sun and
moon come together at twilight. And the fine chariot fighters who are soldiers
of Pandu's son, I will meet them too, with their armies, at the head of the
battle. I have named for you, king, the rathas and atirathas as they are chief,
and some half rathas, and theirs too, lord of the Kurus. I will hold back Arjuna
and Vasudeva, and the other kings there, all those I can see, Bharata.

"'But I would not kill Shikhandi the Panchala prince, strong-armed one, even if
I saw him with his arrow raised, fighting me in battle. The world knows that to
please my father I gave up the kingdom that had come to me and held to my vow of
chastity. I consecrated Chitrangada as king of the Kauravas, and the child
Vichitravirya as crown prince. Having made known my vow as Devavrata on the
earth among all kings, I would never kill a woman, or one who was a woman
before. For Shikhandi was a woman before, king, as you may have heard. He was
born a girl and became a man. I will not fight him, Bharata. I will kill all the
other kings I meet in battle, bull of the Bharatas, but not the sons of Kunti,
king.'"

**Divergences.**

* **Shikhandi.** Bhishma says he will not fight Shikhandi, who was born a girl (5.169.16–20). The story follows in the next chapters (5.170–197). Ledger L-181.
* **Chitrangada.** Bhishma says he consecrated Chitrangada as king and Vichitravirya as crown prince (5.169.18). Book 1 tells of Chitrangada's reign and death before Vichitravirya's; the line here is kept as spoken.
* Rejected lines (\*568–\*569) add detail. Not narrated.

**Choices.**

* 5.167.10–14: hard verses on Virata's and Drupada's kinship and loyalty; the rendering follows their words.
* 5.168.2 *nāśayan pūrvasaṃsthitim*: Shikhandi will fight "destroying his former state", that is, his birth as a girl.
* 5.168.7 *kṣatradharmā*: Kshatradharman, Dhrishtadyumna's son.
