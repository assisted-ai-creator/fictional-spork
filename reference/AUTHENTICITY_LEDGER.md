# Authenticity Ledger

What is really in Vyasa's Mahabharata, what was added later, and what was
never there. Every verdict below was checked against the texts in `sources/`
with `tools/mbh.py`. The evidence column cites the Critical Edition (CE) as
`book.chapter.verse`, CE rejected passages as `*star` or `App.` numbers, and
Ganguli's vulgate translation as `G book.section`.

**This is a living document.** Add an entry whenever a chapter touches a
well-known story. See [How to add an entry](#how-to-add-an-entry).

## Verdicts

| Mark | Verdict | Meaning | In the novel? |
|------|---------|---------|---------------|
| ✅ | **CE** | In the Critical Edition's constituted text. | Yes |
| 🔶 | **CE, but different** | The CE has the episode, but the popular version changes it. | The CE version only |
| ⚠️ | **Later addition** | Only in passages the CE rejected (star/appendix) and/or in the vulgate (Ganguli). Old, and found in some manuscripts, but not the oldest text. | No (endnote only) |
| ❌ | **Not in Vyasa** | In neither the CE, its apparatus lines, nor Ganguli. From later literature, folk tradition or modern fiction. | No (endnote only) |

## How to add an entry

1. **Search** for the episode in the CE, the apparatus, and Ganguli:

   ```bash
   python3 tools/mbh.py check 'SANSKRIT' ['SANSKRIT2'…] -e 'ENGLISH REGEX' [-b BOOK]
   python3 tools/mbh.py search 'word' --all -b BOOK   # CE incl. rejected lines
   python3 tools/mbh.py show 7.121.10-20 --all          # read in context
   python3 tools/mbh.py gsearch 'regex' -b BOOK         # Ganguli
   ```

   Search patterns are "skeletonised": `krishna`, `krsna` and `kṛṣṇa` all
   match. Watch for **sandhi**. A word may be joined to the one before it
   (`dakṣiṇo 'ṅguṣṭho`), so search a fragment (`ngust`), not the whole word.

2. **Read the passage.** A hit count is not evidence. Read the verses and cite
   them. (During this ledger's first build, two claims looked like
   interpolations from their counts but turned out to be in the CE once the
   passages were read: L-44 and L-52.)
3. **Classify** with the four verdicts. "Not found" needs *three* negatives:
   the CE text, the CE apparatus lines (`--all`), and Ganguli.
4. **Attribute** a ❌ entry to its real source only if you have checked it.
   Otherwise write "later tradition".
5. Log the new entry in [`CHANGELOG.md`](../CHANGELOG.md).

### Limits of the evidence

* The GRETIL file holds **many but not all** of the CE's rejected passages.
  A story absent from the CE text *and* the apparatus lines but present in
  Ganguli is still ⚠️. It was simply not keyed into the apparatus file.
* Ganguli translated the Bengal/Bombay vulgate. Southern manuscripts contain
  further additions that neither source shows. A ❌ therefore means "not in
  the CE and not in the vulgate". That is enough to keep it out of the novel.
