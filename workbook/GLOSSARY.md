# Glossary

Sanskrit words the novel keeps, because English has no honest equivalent.
Each is explained **in the sentence** the first time a chapter uses it (see
the Style Guide, §3). The "first-use gloss" column is the house wording. Adapt
it to the sentence, but keep the meaning.

Add a word here before using it in a chapter.

| Word (novel) | IAST | Meaning | First-use gloss (house wording) |
|---|---|---|---|
| dharma | dharma | Right conduct, duty, law, the order that holds the world together; also the god Dharma | "dharma, the right way to live" |
| adharma | adharma | The opposite of dharma: wrong, lawlessness | "adharma, the breaking of what is right" |
| karma | karman | Action, and the fruit that action brings | "karma, the deeds a person does and what they bring back" |
| kshatriya | kṣatriya | The warrior and ruling class | "a kshatriya, one born to rule and fight" |

| shudra | śūdra | The class of labourers and servants | "a shudra, of the serving people" |

| rishi | ṛṣi | A seer, a sage who "sees" sacred truths | "a rishi, a seer" |

| asura | asura | The gods' elder half-brothers and rivals. **Not** "demon" | "the asuras, the gods' old rivals" |
| deva | deva | A god | use "god" |
| gandharva | gandharva | Heavenly musicians and warriors | "a gandharva, one of the singers of heaven" |

| yaksha | yakṣa | Nature spirits, guardians of treasure | "a yaksha, a spirit of the wild places" |
| swayamvara | svayaṃvara | A princess choosing her husband, often by a contest | "a swayamvara, where a princess chooses her own husband" |
| ashrama | āśrama | A hermitage, a forest home of sages | "an ashrama, a forest hermitage" |
| yajna | yajña | A sacrifice: offerings made into sacred fire | "a sacrifice" (use English; keep *yajna* only in names) |
| mantra | mantra | A sacred verse or formula with power | "a mantra, words of power" |

| yojana | yojana | A measure of distance (several miles) | "a yojana, a long day's march" (check each use) |
| Veda | veda | The oldest sacred texts: Rig, Yajur, Sama (and Atharva) | "the Vedas, the oldest sacred hymns" |
| amrita | amṛta | The drink of immortality | "amrita, the nectar that makes the gods deathless" |
| soma | soma | The sacred drink offered in sacrifice | "soma, the drink of the gods" |
| Brahmastra | brahmāstra / brahmaśiras | The weapon of Brahma, most terrible of all divine weapons | "the Brahma weapon" |
| astra | astra | A divine weapon, called up with a mantra | "a divine weapon" |
| sannyasa | saṃnyāsa | Renunciation of the world | "renunciation" |
| moksha | mokṣa | Release from the cycle of birth and death | "moksha, release" |
| Kali Yuga (and the other yugas) | kaliyuga, dvāpara | The four ages of the world, declining in virtue | "the Kali age, the last and worst of the four ages" |

| nirvana | nirvāṇa | The "blowing out": peace, release | "nirvana, the peace of release" |
