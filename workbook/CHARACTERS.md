# Character Bible

Who the people are **as the Critical Edition shows them**: their origin,
nature, relationships and key moments, each with a verse reference. This
guards against the most common drift in retellings, which is importing a
character's later reputation (from plays, films and novels) into the epic.

Rules:

* Describe people by **what they do and say in the text**, and by how the
  text itself characterises them. No psychological theories.
* Where the text is ambiguous or contradicts itself, record both readings with refs.
* Add to an entry whenever a chapter shows something new. Always give the ref.

Spellings follow [`NAMES.md`](NAMES.md).

## The divine portions (CE 1.61)

demons and spirits were born as which people (1.61.63–98). The novel narrates
this list (Book 1). It is the text's own framing of the cast:

| Bhishma | the youngest of the eight Vasus, born to Ganga and Shantanu by Vasishtha's curse | 1.61.68–70 |

## The main cast

Each entry grows as the drafting reaches that person. **Bold** items still
need refs.

### Bhishma
* Devavrata, eighth son of Shantanu and Ganga; the one Vasu who stayed (1.61.68–69).
* Vows before the kings to give up the throne and never have children, so
  that his father can marry Satyavati (1.94.86–88). The gods name him *Bhishma* (1.94.90).
* Given by his father the power to die only when he chooses (1.94.94).
* The text calls him wise, learned in the Vedas, eloquent, the terror of
  his enemies (1.61.69). He fought Rama Jamadagnya (1.61.70).

### Draupadi
* Born from the middle of the sacrificial altar in Drupada's house (1.61.95).
  Described there: neither too short nor too tall, fragrant as the blue
  lotus, with eyes like lotus petals, long dark hair, and a colour like
  cat's-eye gem (1.61.96–97).
* **Birth scene and Drupada's sacrifice**: 1.155 (to confirm while drafting).
* Speaks with force and reason throughout. In the CE she does **not**
  reject Karna at the swayamvara (ledger L-12), does **not** say "blind man's
  son" (L-17), and makes **no** vow about washing her hair in blood (L-22).

### Karna
* Kunti's first son, by the Sun, born wearing armour (1.61.89).
* Raised by Radha and the suta Adhiratha (3.293.2–10).
* Cuts off his armour and gives it to Indra (3.294.38).
* In the CE he fails at the swayamvara bow like the other kings (1.179.4).
  Draupadi's public rejection is not in the CE (L-12).
* Cursed by his teacher Rama Jamadagnya for deceiving him: the Brahma weapon
  will fail him (12.3.30–31). The earth swallows his wheel (8.66.59).

### Duryodhana
* Eldest son of Dhritarashtra and Gandhari; from a portion of Kali (1.61.80–81).
* Bares his thigh to Draupadi in the hall (2.63.12). Bhima vows to break it
  (2.63.14). Maitreya curses him to the same end (3.11.34).
* His wife is never named in the CE (L-60).

### Krishna
* Vasudeva, from a portion of Narayana (1.61.90).
* Rushes at Bhishma twice: with the discus (6.55.86–92) and with his whip
  (6.102.53–54).
* In the CE he does **not** hide the sun at Jayadratha's death (L-40).
  Draupadi is **not** shown praying to him at the disrobing (L-19).

### Yudhishthira
* Dharma's son (1.61.84).
* Says "Ashvatthama is dead", adding "the elephant" indistinctly. His
  chariot, which always stayed four fingers above the ground, touches it
  (7.164.106–107).
* Curses all women never to keep a secret, on learning Karna was his
  brother (12.6.10).
* Refuses to enter heaven without the dog that followed him (17.3.7–20).
