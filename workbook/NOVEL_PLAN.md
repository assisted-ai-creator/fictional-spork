# Novel Plan

**Working title:** *The Mahabharata: Vyasa's Epic, Told in Plain English*
(to be decided; see [Open questions](#open-questions))

**Shape:** 18 Books, one for each parva of the Critical Edition, in the CE's
order. Every CE adhyāya is covered by exactly one chapter.
[`index/COVERAGE.md`](../index/COVERAGE.md) tracks this automatically.

**Estimated length:** about 430 chapters, 1.2–1.5 million words. Narrative
parvas are told scene by scene. The long teaching sections (Śānti,
Anuśāsana) are told as dialogues with their stories kept, more compressed,
but with no teaching left out.

## How chapters are cut

* A chapter covers a **continuous CE range** (e.g. `1.94–1.95`), usually 2–6
  adhyāyas, and ends at a natural turn in the story.
* Inset tales (Śakuntalā, Nala, Sāvitrī, Rāma…) get their own chapters.
  They open by naming who is telling the tale to whom.
* Battle books follow the **days** of the war, marked by dawn and dusk.
  Days are mapped in [`TIMELINE.md`](TIMELINE.md) as each book is drafted.
* The detailed chapter list for a Book is written just before drafting it,
  by reading the CE. Estimates for later Books are provisional.

## The eighteen Books

Sub-parva (upaparva) ranges are the CE's own divisions. **Book 1 is
confirmed.** For the other Books the ranges are *provisional*: the
concordance gives Ganguli's vulgate divisions, and these must be checked
against the CE when the Book is drafted.

## Open questions

These are for the author to decide. The defaults are shown.

1. **Title.** Default: *The Mahabharata: Vyasa's Epic, Told in Plain English*.
2. **Vulgate companion.** Should famous rejected episodes (Gaṇeśa, Kṛṣṇa
   at the disrobing, Urvaśī's curse) appear in boxed sidebars in a separate
   edition? Default: no, endnotes only (see Canon Policy §7).
3. **The Gītā.** Default: told in full as dialogue, all eighteen chapters, in the
   house style, with no teaching dropped.
4. **Endnotes in print.** Default: gathered at the back of each Book.
