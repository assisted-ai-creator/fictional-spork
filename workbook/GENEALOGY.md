## The Kuru line: Shantanu to Janamejaya

```
                          Shantanu ═══ Ganga
                              ║
                     Devavrata = BHISHMA (vowed celibate, 1.94.88)

  Parashara ─ Satyavati ═══ Shantanu
      │            ║
    VYASA   ┌──────┴────────────┐
      ¦     Chitrangada     Vichitravirya ═══ Ambika, Ambalika (princesses of Kashi)
      ¦     (died young)    (died young, childless)
      ¦
      ¦ (at Satyavati's request, Vyasa fathers sons for his brother's line)
      ├── with Ambika ──────────► DHRITARASHTRA (born blind)
      ├── with Ambalika ────────► PANDU (born pale)
      └── with a serving woman ─► VIDURA (Dharma born as a man)

  DHRITARASHTRA ═══ Gandhari (princess of Gandhara, sister of Shakuni)
      ├── Duryodhana (eldest), Duhshasana, Vikarna … one hundred sons
      ├── Duhshala (one daughter) ═══ Jayadratha
      └── Yuyutsu (by a vaishya woman)

  PANDU ═══ Kunti (Pritha)                  PANDU ═══ Madri (sister of Shalya)
      (Kunti's sons by the gods,               (Madri's sons by the Ashvins)
       at Pandu's wish)
      ├── Yudhishthira  (by Dharma)            ├── Nakula
      ├── Bhima         (by Vayu, the wind)    └── Sahadeva
      └── Arjuna        (by Indra)

  Kunti's first son, born before her marriage:
      Karna (by Surya, the Sun), raised by Adhiratha the suta and his wife Radha

  The five Pandavas ═══ Draupadi (daughter of Drupada)
      ├── Prativindhya (Yudhishthira's son)   ├── Sutasoma (Bhima's)
      ├── Shrutakarma (Arjuna's)             ├── Shatanika (Nakula's)
      └── Shrutasena (Sahadeva's)                                   (ref)

  Other sons:
      Bhima ═══ Hidimba (rakshasi) ─► Ghatotkacha
      Arjuna ═══ Ulupi (Naga princess) ─► Iravan
      Arjuna ═══ Chitrangada (princess of Manipura) ─► Babhruvahana
      Arjuna ═══ Subhadra (Krishna's sister) ─► ABHIMANYU ═══ Uttara (Virata's daughter)
                                                  └─► PARIKSHIT ─► JANAMEJAYA
```

## Kunti's and Madri's families

```
  Shura (a Yadava) ─┬─ Pritha = KUNTI (given to Kuntibhoja, who raised her)
                    └─ Vasudeva ═══ Devaki ─► KRISHNA; Balarama; (Subhadra) (ref)

  So Krishna is the Pandavas' cousin: Kunti is his father's sister.   (ref)

  Madra royal house: Shalya (king) and his sister Madri
```
